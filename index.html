The Story of Silk
01 The moth

Where silk begins

This is where
silk begins.

Tiny eggs rest
on mulberry leaves.

The silkworm eats.
And grows.

One long thread
becomes a cocoon.

The cocoon opens.
Silk is found.

Each thread
takes on colour.

Warp meets weft.
Cloth takes shape.

From a small cocoon
comes silk.

The journey begins with a small silk moth.
